Planning a Mile End Project

Making access, parking and day-to-day living part of the build plan

Mile End projects need a plan that works for the property as well as the proposed new room. Before pricing, we look at how materials can reach the work area, where waste can be handled, whether the household needs to retain parking and which services cross the proposed footprint. These practical checks help turn an attractive drawing into a buildable scope.

For an attached or integral garage, we consider the floor level, insulation, ventilation and the connection into the main house. For a rear extension, the early questions include drainage, garden access, natural light and how the new room will relate to the existing kitchen. Internal alterations may also need structural calculations and a clear sequence for temporary support and making good.

A written quote should distinguish structural work from customer choices such as flooring, kitchen units, sanitaryware and decoration. That makes it easier for Mile End homeowners to compare proposals on the same basis and understand which decisions are still outstanding.
