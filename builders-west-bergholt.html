Planning a West Bergholt Project

Respecting the existing house while improving space, warmth and flow

West Bergholt properties vary in age, construction and setting, so a standard extension specification is rarely enough. Earlier alterations, different wall construction and changes in floor level can all influence how new work should connect to the house. An initial survey should identify those conditions before the final layout and finishes are fixed.

External materials deserve particular attention on a village home. Brickwork, roof form, window proportions and rainwater details should be considered together so the extension sits comfortably with the existing building. Where planning permission is required, resolving that appearance early also gives drawings and quotations a consistent basis.

For larger refurbishment projects, we plan the sequence around weather protection, structural openings and service renewals before kitchens, bathrooms and final finishes begin. This reduces the risk of completed work being damaged and makes it clearer whether the household can remain safely in the property during each stage.
