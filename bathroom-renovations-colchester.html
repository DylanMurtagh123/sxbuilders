Bathroom renovation costs

What Affects the Cost of a Bathroom Renovation in Colchester?

Bathroom renovation costs vary depending on the size of the room, the existing condition, the amount of plumbing work needed and the level of finish you want. A straightforward bathroom refurbishment will usually be different to a full bathroom remodel, ensuite renovation, shower room upgrade or wet room-style project.

We look at the room properly before quoting so you understand what is included, what preparation is needed and how the work will be planned around your home.

Free quote: Tell us whether you want a full bathroom renovation, small bathroom refurbishment, ensuite upgrade, walk-in shower or wet room-style finish and we’ll give clear advice on the work involved.

Room size and layout

Small bathrooms, ensuites, family bathrooms and shower rooms all need different levels of preparation and fitting work.

Plumbing changes

Moving toilets, showers, baths, basins or radiators can affect the amount of work needed before the finish goes in.

Tiling and flooring

Wall tiling, floor tiling, surface preparation, waterproofing and flooring choices can all influence the final budget.

Fixtures and fittings

The choice of shower, bath, vanity unit, taps, screens, storage and accessories affects both the look and cost.

Lighting and ventilation

Bathrooms need practical lighting and suitable ventilation, especially for shower rooms, ensuites and busy family spaces.

Final finish

Plastering, trims, sealant, decorating and finishing details help the bathroom feel clean, complete and professionally fitted.
