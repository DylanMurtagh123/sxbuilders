Project costs

What affects the cost of building work in Lexden?

Every Lexden building project is different, but these are the main factors that usually affect the final quote.

Project size and scope

A single bathroom refit, garage conversion, kitchen renovation and full extension all require different levels of labour, materials and project management.

Older property condition

Character homes may need extra care around existing structure, floors, walls, drainage, insulation, ventilation or hidden issues found once work begins.

Access, parking and driveways

Working around established roads, driveways, gardens and neighbouring properties can affect deliveries, waste removal and how the project is organised.

Structural work

Open-plan layouts, knock-throughs, steels, foundations and roof changes can increase cost because they need careful planning and skilled work.

Kitchens, bathrooms and services

Plumbing, electrics, heating, tiling, flooring, lighting and ventilation can all affect the price, especially where the layout is changing.

Finish level and materials

The choice of fixtures, fittings, flooring, units, worktops, tiles and decoration will influence the final cost and overall finish.
