Cost Factors

What affects the cost of building work in Colchester?

The cost of a home extension, garage conversion, renovation, kitchen or bathroom project depends on the property, the level of finish and the amount of preparation needed. We provide clear written quotes so you understand what is included before work begins.

Project size and scopeA full renovation or extension naturally involves more labour, materials and time than a single room upgrade.
Access and parkingCity centre streets, terraces and tight driveways can affect deliveries, skips, working areas and scheduling.
Structural workKnock-throughs, steels, foundations, drainage and roof changes can change the level of building work required.
Older property considerationsPeriod homes may need extra care around existing walls, floors, services, damp, ventilation and previous alterations.
Specification and finishTiles, flooring, worktops, sanitaryware, windows, doors, fixtures and fittings all affect the final quote.
Plumbing and electricsKitchens, bathrooms, utility areas and garage conversions often need extra preparation, wiring, lighting and pipework.
