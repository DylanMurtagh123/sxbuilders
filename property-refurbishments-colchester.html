Property Refurbishment FAQs

Questions homeowners ask before refurbishing a property

Clear answers for homeowners considering a full house refurbishment, partial renovation or property upgrade in Colchester.

Do you carry out property refurbishments in Colchester?

Yes. Essex Builders carry out property refurbishments in Colchester, including full house refurbishments, partial renovations, layout improvements, kitchen and bathroom work, plastering, flooring and finishing.

What is included in a property refurbishment?

A property refurbishment can include strip-out work, repairs, layout changes, structural alterations, plastering, flooring, kitchen renovation, bathroom renovation, joinery, making good and finishing work. The exact scope depends on the condition of the property and the result you want.

Can you refurbish a house before I move in?

Yes. Many homeowners arrange refurbishment work before moving into a property so the main repairs, layout changes, kitchens, bathrooms and finishing work can be completed before the home is used every day.

Can refurbishment work include structural alterations?

Yes. Refurbishment projects can include structural alterations such as wall removals, wider openings and layout changes where suitable. Load-bearing work may require structural calculations and building control approval.

Can you update kitchens and bathrooms as part of a refurbishment?

Yes. Kitchen and bathroom upgrades are often included in wider property refurbishments, along with plumbing, electrics, plastering, flooring and finishing work.

Can you help with older properties?

Yes. Older homes often need a careful approach because existing plaster, floors, services, previous alterations, damp, ventilation and structure can all affect how the work should be carried out.

How much does a property refurbishment cost in Colchester?

The cost depends on the property size, condition, access, strip-out work, structural changes, kitchen and bathroom specification, plastering, flooring, electrics, plumbing, materials and finish level. A site visit and written quote is the best way to price the work properly.

Can you quote if I already have drawings, survey notes or a snagging list?

Yes. If you already have drawings, survey notes, a snagging list or a clear list of required work, Essex Builders can review the information and provide a quote based on the agreed scope.
