Planning, Structure & Building Regulations

Kitchen extensions need careful building work behind the finished look

The finished kitchen is what everyone sees, but the quality of the extension depends on the work behind it. Foundations, drainage, roof construction, insulation, structural openings, steelwork, ventilation, electrics, plumbing and glazing all need to be considered properly.

Essex Builders can discuss the practical requirements of the project and quote for the building work involved. If you already have drawings, plans or structural details, these can be reviewed before the work is priced.

  • Planning permission can depend on the size, position and type of property
  • Building regulations commonly apply to extensions, structure, insulation and services
  • Open-plan kitchen extensions may need steelwork or load-bearing wall alterations
  • Drainage, foundations, access and glazing choices can affect the scope and cost

What affects the cost of a kitchen extension?

The price of a kitchen extension depends on the building work required and the finish you want to achieve. A simple rear extension will not be priced the same as a large open-plan kitchen-diner with structural alterations, rooflights and a high-spec kitchen.

Kitchen Extension FAQs

Questions homeowners ask before building a kitchen extension

Clear answers for homeowners considering a kitchen extension, open-plan kitchen-diner or rear extension in Colchester.

Do you build kitchen extensions in Colchester?

Yes. Essex Builders build kitchen extensions in Colchester, including rear kitchen extensions, side extensions, open-plan kitchen-diners and single storey kitchen extension projects.

Can you create an open-plan kitchen-diner?

Yes. Many kitchen extensions involve opening up the back of the house to create a larger kitchen, dining and living space. This can include structural openings, steelwork, plastering and preparation for the kitchen installation.

What types of kitchen extensions can you help with?

We can help with rear kitchen extensions, side extensions, kitchen-diner extensions, utility extensions and kitchen extensions that form part of a wider home renovation or ground-floor layout change.

Do I need planning permission for a kitchen extension?

Some kitchen extensions may fall under permitted development, while others need planning permission. This depends on the size, position, property type, previous extensions and any local restrictions. It is always worth checking the correct route before work starts.

Will my kitchen extension need building regulations approval?

Most kitchen extensions need building regulations approval. This usually covers foundations, drainage, structure, roof construction, insulation, ventilation, electrics, plumbing, fire safety and glazing.

Can you help with structural work for a kitchen extension?

Yes. Kitchen extension work often involves removing walls, creating larger openings and installing steelwork where the existing home connects to the extension. Essex Builders can discuss the building work required for the project.

Can you work from drawings or plans?

Yes. If you already have architectural drawings, planning documents or structural calculations, Essex Builders can review the details and provide a quote for the building work involved.

How much does a kitchen extension cost in Colchester?

Cost depends on the extension size, foundations, drainage, access, roof type, glazing, structural work, electrics, plumbing, insulation, kitchen specification and finish level. A site visit and written quote is the best way to price the work properly.
