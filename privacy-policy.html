Who we are

Essex Builders

Essex Builders provides building services across Colchester and the surrounding Essex areas, including home extensions, garage conversions, home renovations, kitchen renovations and bathroom renovations.

For the purpose of this privacy policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refers to Essex Builders. This policy applies to personal information collected through this website, by phone, by email, through WhatsApp or through our contact and quote enquiry forms.

Contact details: You can contact Essex Builders by phone on 07958 355936 or by email at sxgroup@yahoo.co.uk.

Information we collect

Personal Information We May Collect

When you contact Essex Builders or request a quote, we may collect the information needed to respond to your enquiry and understand the building work you are interested in.

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your property location or the area where the work is required.
  • Details about your project, such as whether you are interested in a home extension, garage conversion, renovation, kitchen renovation or bathroom renovation.
  • Messages, images or documents you choose to send us about your project.
  • Basic website usage information where analytics tools are used, such as pages visited, approximate location, referral source, device type, browser information, session information and interactions with the website.

We only ask for information that is relevant to your enquiry, quote request, project communication or use of our website.

How we use information

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, manage customer communication and deliver building services.

  • To respond when you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp or a website form.
  • To arrange site visits, quote appointments and project discussions.
  • To prepare and send quotes for building work.
  • To communicate with you before, during and after a project.
  • To keep records relating to enquiries, quotes, invoices, payments and completed work.
  • To improve our website, services and customer experience.
  • To understand how visitors find and use the website, including which pages are most useful and where enquiries may come from.
  • To measure website performance, marketing activity and general enquiry trends.
  • To comply with legal, accounting or business record requirements.

Our lawful basis for using your information

We usually process personal information because it is necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, take steps before entering into a contract, carry out agreed work, meet legal obligations or pursue legitimate business interests such as managing enquiries and customer records.

Sharing information

When We May Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information only when needed to respond to your enquiry, manage a project, run the business or meet legal requirements.

  • With trusted trades, subcontractors or suppliers where this is needed for your project.
  • With professional advisers such as accountants, bookkeepers or legal advisers.
  • With service providers that help operate our website, email, phone, hosting, forms or business systems.
  • With analytics and website service providers, such as Google Analytics, where used to measure website performance and visitor behaviour.
  • With authorities, regulators or other parties where required by law.

Where we share information, we only share what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Data retention

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information for as long as needed to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, manage work, maintain business records and comply with legal, tax or accounting requirements.

If you make an enquiry but do not proceed with work, we may keep your enquiry details for a reasonable period in case you come back to us or need follow-up information. If you become a customer, we may keep project and invoice records for longer where required for business, warranty, accounting or legal reasons.

Cookies and website tools

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, scripts and similar technologies to help the website work properly, understand website performance and improve the experience for visitors.

Cookies are small files or identifiers that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Some cookies are necessary for basic website functions, while others may help us understand how people use the site.

  • Essential cookies: used where needed for the website to function correctly.
  • Analytics cookies: used to help us understand how visitors use the website, which pages are viewed and how the website can be improved.
  • Third-party tools: used where the website includes services such as Google Analytics, Google Fonts, WhatsApp links, phone links, email links or map-related features.

You can usually manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain parts of the website work. If a cookie banner or cookie settings tool is shown on the website, you can use it to manage your choices.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to help us understand how visitors use the site and how people find our pages. Google Analytics helps us measure website performance, improve content, understand which services and areas are most useful to visitors, and make better decisions about the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, interactions with the website, browser and device information, approximate location, referral source and general session data. This information is used for reporting and analysis and does not directly identify you to us.

Google may process this information according to its own privacy and data processing terms. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use Google services by visiting Google’s privacy and partner sites information pages.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and may also use Google’s available tools or browser settings to limit analytics tracking where applicable.

Security

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to keep personal information secure and protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access. This includes limiting access to personal information and using appropriate business systems for communication and record keeping.

No website or email system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information unless it is necessary for your enquiry or project.

Your rights

Your Data Protection Rights

You have rights over your personal information. Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information where appropriate.
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent to use your information.

To make a request, please contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before responding to certain requests.

If you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled, you can contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue. You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Updates

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, business practices or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date shown near the top.
