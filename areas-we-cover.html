Questions About Areas Covered

What areas do Essex Builders cover?

We are based in Colchester and cover nearby Essex areas including Mile End, Highwoods, West Bergholt, Prettygate, Shrub End, Stanway, Lexden, Wivenhoe, Tiptree, West Mersea, Marks Tey, Kelvedon, Witham, Maldon, Clacton-on-Sea, Manningtree and surrounding villages.

Do you only work in Colchester?

No. Essex Builders are Colchester-based, but cover a range of nearby Essex towns, villages and neighbourhoods depending on the project.

Can I still enquire if my area is not listed?

Yes. If you are near Colchester or within the wider Essex area, send your project details and location and we can confirm whether we can help.

Do you cover all services in every area?

The main services are home extensions, garage conversions, home renovations, kitchen renovations and bathroom renovations. Availability can depend on project size, access and location.

Can you help with more than one service in the same project?

Yes. Many projects include more than one service, such as an extension with a kitchen renovation, a garage conversion with internal renovation work, or a bathroom upgrade as part of a wider refurbishment.

Do you cover smaller nearby villages?

Often, yes. We can usually discuss projects in nearby neighbourhoods and villages such as Mile End, Highwoods, West Bergholt, Prettygate, Shrub End, Great Horkesley, Dedham, Brightlingsea, Rowhedge, Copford, Eight Ash Green, Ardleigh and Elmstead Market.
