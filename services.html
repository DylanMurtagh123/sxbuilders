What Affects the Cost of Building Work in Colchester?

Costs vary because every home, layout and finish is different. A home extension, garage conversion, property refurbishment, structural alteration, wall removal, kitchen renovation or bathroom refurbishment will all be priced around the actual work required, not a one-size-fits-all estimate.

We look at the property, access, structure, materials, plumbing, electrics, finish level and any planning or building regulations considerations before giving a clear written quote.
