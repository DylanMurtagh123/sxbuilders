Costs & Planning

What affects the cost of building work in Wivenhoe?

Every project is different, but these are the main factors that can influence the quote for extensions, renovations, garage conversions, kitchens and bathrooms.

Project size and complexity

A small bathroom renovation will be priced differently to a full home renovation, structural knock-through, garage conversion or larger home extension.

Property age and condition

Older or riverside homes may need extra care around existing walls, floors, damp, services, levels, access and finishes.

Access and parking

Narrower streets, limited parking, garden access and delivery space can affect planning, labour time and how materials are moved safely.

Structural work

Opening walls, adding steels, changing layouts or creating larger openings can affect the cost and may require additional professional input.

Kitchens, bathrooms and services

Plumbing, electrics, heating, ventilation, tiling, flooring and appliance choices all influence the final price.

Planning and building regulations

Some projects may need planning permission, permitted development checks or building control approval depending on the property and the work involved.
