Renovation cost factors

What Affects the Cost of a Home Renovation in Colchester?

Every house renovation is different, so the cost depends on the size of the property, the condition of the existing building and the level of finish you want. We provide clear written quotes so you understand what is included before work begins.

Scope of the Renovation

A single room refurbishment will usually cost less than a full home renovation involving several rooms, structural changes, kitchens, bathrooms and finishing work.

Condition of the Property

Older homes, dated interiors, uneven floors, tired plaster, damp issues or hidden defects can affect the amount of preparation and repair work required.

Layout Changes

Internal alterations, knock-throughs, new openings, structural support, moving services and changing room layouts can all influence the overall renovation quote.

Kitchens and Bathrooms

Kitchen renovations and bathroom renovations often involve plumbing, electrics, tiling, flooring, fitting and a higher level of finishing detail.

Materials and Finish Level

Flooring, tiles, units, fixtures, fittings, lighting and final decorative choices all affect the budget and the final look of the renovated space.

Access and Timescale

Working around occupied homes, restricted access, staged renovation work or tight deadlines can affect how the project is planned and priced.
