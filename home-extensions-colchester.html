Every extension is priced around the property, the structure and the finish you want. A simple single-storey rear extension will be very different from a larger double-storey build, kitchen extension or project involving drainage, steels, glazing and internal layout changes.

We keep quotes clear and practical, so you can understand what is included before making decisions. During your enquiry, we will talk through the size, access, build complexity and finish level so the quote reflects the actual project rather than a vague estimate.

FAQs

Home Extension Questions

Here are a few common questions from homeowners planning an extension in Colchester.

Do you build home extensions in Colchester?

Yes. Essex Builders build home extensions across Colchester and nearby Essex areas, including single-storey, double-storey, rear, side and kitchen extensions.

Do I need planning permission for a home extension?

Some extensions may fall under permitted development, while others may need planning permission. This depends on the property, size, design and location. We can discuss the likely route during your quote.

Can you build a kitchen extension?

Yes. Kitchen extensions are one of the most popular ways to create a brighter, more practical family space with better dining, storage and open-plan living.

How much does a home extension cost in Colchester?

The cost depends on size, storeys, groundwork, access, structural requirements, glazing, plumbing, electrics and the level of finish. We provide clear quotes after discussing your property and the type of extension you want.

Do home extensions need building regulations approval?

Most home extensions need to meet building regulations for areas such as foundations, structure, insulation, ventilation, electrics, drainage, fire safety and energy performance. We can discuss the likely requirements during your quote.

Should I choose a single-storey or double-storey extension?

A single-storey extension is ideal for larger kitchens, dining areas and family living space, while a double-storey extension can add both downstairs space and upstairs bedrooms or bathrooms. The best option depends on your property, budget, planning route and how much extra space you need.

Is a home extension better than a garage conversion?

A home extension is usually best when you need brand-new living space, a larger kitchen or a bigger layout change. A garage conversion can be a more practical option when you already have unused garage space. We can help you compare both options during your quote.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for home extension projects across Colchester and surrounding Essex areas.

Do you cover areas outside Colchester?

Yes. We cover Colchester and surrounding areas including Stanway, Lexden, Wivenhoe, Tiptree, Marks Tey, Kelvedon, Witham, Maldon, West Mersea, Manningtree and Clacton-on-Sea.

Can you help with renovation work alongside the extension?

Yes. Many extension projects involve renovation work, kitchens, bathrooms, knock-throughs, plastering, flooring and finishing details. We can discuss the full scope when quoting.
